# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, welcoming, and careful place for contributors. The app concerns meditation, spiritual learning, personal reflection, and potentially sensitive user data, so the community must handle both people and content with care.

We pledge to make participation in this project free from harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or disrespectful behavior.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that helps the project:

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Discuss ideas directly without attacking people.
- Keep spiritual, cultural, and personal topics careful and humble.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- Mark uncertain claims clearly.
- Respect user privacy and sensitive data.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project context.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or conduct
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing private information without consent
- Dismissing source, licensing, privacy, or safety concerns
- Presenting unverified spiritual or historical claims as authoritative
- Repeatedly derailing technical or content review discussions
- Spam or promotional contributions unrelated to the project

## Content and Spiritual Care

This project may include references to Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi, Sahaja Yoga, mantras, talks, meditations, and personal practice. Contributors should treat these topics respectfully and avoid making claims that are not sourced or reviewed.

Content that is uncertain should be marked as `draft` or `needs_review`. Direct quotes, translations, dates, and source attributions should be verified before being treated as approved.

## Privacy Care

The app may include journaling, favorites, usage history, and later community features. Contributors must avoid exposing personal data, logs, tokens, or private notes.

Privacy concerns should be treated as valid even when they complicate implementation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that harms contributors, users, or the project.

## Reporting

A private reporting contact has not yet been published. Until then, please avoid sharing sensitive reports publicly. A security and conduct contact should be added before the project invites broader community participation.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by common open-source community standards and adapted for this project's privacy, content, and spiritual-source responsibilities.
